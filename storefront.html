03 / STOREFRONT

How the storefront is organized

Khavaro combines Shopify's native commerce architecture with a visual language designed for premium fashion presentation.

Header

The header provides brand identity, primary navigation and access to storefront utilities such as search, customer accounts and cart functionality where enabled by the store configuration.

Mega menu

Desktop navigation can expose deeper category structures through the mega menu. Keep the hierarchy meaningful and avoid excessive levels. Mobile navigation is presented through a separate drawer experience.

Homepage

The supplied homepage is editorial and merchandising-oriented. Its main composition includes a promotional ticker, hero, campaign/promotion content, new arrivals, category navigation, editorial content, brand storytelling and newsletter/footer content. Individual sections can be reordered, removed or configured in the Theme Editor according to the merchant's needs.

Product pages

Product pages are built around Shopify's product data. Product media, variants, prices, availability and purchase actions come from the store's product configuration. Additional information can be presented through collapsible content blocks and related-product merchandising.

Collection pages

Collection pages are intended to scale from small curated assortments to larger catalogs. Merchants should test filtering, sorting, pagination and empty-state behavior using the actual catalog and market configuration of the store.

Cart

Khavaro uses Shopify's cart architecture for cart updates and checkout progression. Test quantity changes, removing items, discounts, selling plans where applicable and the transition to checkout before publishing.

Responsive behavior

The theme is designed for desktop and mobile storefronts. Review navigation, product grids, hero imagery, buttons, text wrapping and horizontal controls at narrow viewport widths. Do not assume a desktop layout will automatically communicate the same way on mobile.

App blocks

Where supported by the relevant product sections, Shopify app blocks can be used to integrate compatible app content. App functionality remains dependent on the app and its own configuration; the theme should not require an app to perform its core commerce functions.

Theme Editor workflow

  1. Open the template or page you want to modify.
  2. Add, remove or reorder sections using Shopify's editor.
  3. Open a section to adjust its content and presentation settings.
  4. Preview desktop and mobile layouts.
  5. Save and publish only after storefront testing is complete.
