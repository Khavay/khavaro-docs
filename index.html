THEME AT A GLANCE

Designed for merchant control

Khavaro keeps the storefront visually deliberate while exposing the important merchant-facing controls through Shopify's Theme Editor. Images, collections, navigation, typography, colors and section content are intended to be configurable without editing theme code.
