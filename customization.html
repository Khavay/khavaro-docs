02 / CUSTOMIZATION

Shape the storefront

Most visual and merchandising decisions should be made in Shopify's Theme Editor. Keep code editing for advanced development work only.

Global theme settings

AreaWhat to configure
LogoBrand logo, logo width and favicon.
ColorsGlobal color schemes for backgrounds, text, buttons, borders and supporting UI.
TypographyHeading and body font selections and scale controls.
LayoutPage width, section spacing and grid spacing.
ButtonsBorder thickness, opacity, radius and shadow behavior.
CardsProduct, collection and blog card presentation, alignment, borders, radius and shadows.
MediaMedia borders, radius and presentation behavior.

Homepage sections

Hero

The hero presentation provides an editorial first impression with eyebrow text, heading, supporting copy, calls to action and imagery. Use the Theme Editor to replace the supplied visual content with the merchant's own campaign assets.

New Arrivals

Select the collection to merchandise, choose the product limit and configure the section heading and view-all destination. The section is designed for responsive presentation and desktop horizontal navigation where applicable.

Category split

Use the category split to direct shoppers toward major collections or curated storefront destinations. Configure the labels, links and associated navigation resources in the section settings.

Editorial, brand story and lookbook

These sections are intended for brand positioning and visual storytelling. Replace the supplied text and imagery with content that accurately represents the merchant's brand and products.

Newsletter

Configure the newsletter section as the primary email-capture point for editorial updates, launches or other legitimate marketing communications. Connect it to the store's chosen Shopify-compatible customer/newsletter workflow.

Header and mega menu

The header supports Shopify navigation resources and a desktop mega-menu presentation. Keep the navigation hierarchy concise. For a fashion store, a practical structure is usually a small number of top-level categories followed by focused subcategories.

Product page

The product template uses Shopify's product object and supports product details, variants, media, purchase controls and related products. Use the collapsible content blocks for merchant-specific information such as materials, care, fit, sizing and shipping/returns.

Collection page

The collection template presents a collection hero and product grid with collection merchandising controls. Configure collection content in Shopify admin and use the Theme Editor for presentation-level settings.

Localization and markets

Use Shopify's native localization, markets, currency and customer-account configuration. Avoid hardcoding storefront-specific currency, product or collection destinations when configuring merchant content.

Merchant content rule: Replace any supplied example copy that describes shipping thresholds, returns, press mentions, manufacturing locations, sustainability claims or other factual business statements unless those statements are true for your store.
