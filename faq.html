Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common merchant questions about installing, configuring and maintaining Khavaro.

Do I need to edit code to configure Khavaro?

No. Normal merchant setup is intended to be completed through Shopify admin and the Theme Editor. Code editing is for advanced development and maintenance.

Can I use my own products and collections?

Yes. Product and collection merchandising is based on Shopify's native resources. Assign the appropriate collections and verify product availability, variants and media.

Can I change the typography and colors?

Yes. Use the global theme settings and relevant section settings exposed by the Theme Editor.

Does Khavaro support mobile storefronts?

Yes. The storefront is designed to be responsive, with a dedicated mobile navigation experience. Review the final storefront on actual mobile viewport sizes before publishing.

Does Khavaro require an app?

Core storefront and commerce functionality should not depend on an app. Compatible app blocks can be used where the relevant Shopify section supports them.

What information should I send with a support request?

Include the store URL, theme version, affected page or template, reproduction steps, expected behavior, actual behavior and screenshots or recordings when useful. Never include credentials.

How quickly are support requests answered?

Khavaro support follows a target of responding within two business days.

Where is the support form?

Use the public Khavaro Support Form. You can also reach khavay.support@gmail.com.
