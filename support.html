06 / SUPPORT

Khavaro support

Use the public support form for theme-related questions, bug reports and configuration issues.

Public support form

The support form is the primary support channel. It is designed to collect the information needed to investigate a storefront issue.

Open Support FormEmail Support

What to include

  • First and last name.
  • Email address.
  • Store URL, for example https://your-store.myshopify.com.
  • Khavaro theme version.
  • Affected page, template or section.
  • Clear description of the problem.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue.
  • Screenshots or other useful files when relevant.

Support policy

Khavay provides support for Khavaro theme-related questions and technical issues. The target response time is within two business days. Critical technical issues are prioritized.

Security

Never submit credentials. Do not include Shopify passwords, private access tokens, API keys, staff credentials or other secrets in a support request.

Before submitting

  • Confirm the issue occurs on the storefront, not only in an editor preview.
  • Confirm the affected product, collection, menu or resource exists and is published as expected.
  • Record the exact steps that reproduce the issue.
  • Capture a screenshot if the problem is visual.
